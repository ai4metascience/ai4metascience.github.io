Venue allocation

Sunday, December 13, 2026. Talks in Amphitheater 55B, Sorbonne University (Jussieu), 4 Pl. Jussieu, 75005 Paris. Capacity 120 theater, 2 wireless microphones. Posters and coffee in Caves Esclangon, 40 poster boards, a 5 to 8 minute walk from the lecture room.

NeurIPS fixes the following for our slot (schedule type A) and states that no changes are possible:

  • Workshop hours 8:30 to 12:15, then 13:15 to 18:15.
  • Lunch 12:15 to 13:15.
  • Caves Esclangon is ours only 10:00 to 11:30 and 13:30 to 15:30. The SLM-Agents workshop uses the same room 11:30 to 13:00.
  • Coffee is served 10:00 to 12:00 in the morning and 14:30 to 15:30 in the afternoon.

Every item below sits inside those windows, and each poster session is followed by a 10 minute buffer to walk back to the lecture room.

Open questions for the committee

  • Lunch location is not specified. The NeurIPS planning sheet gives Jussieu workshops a lunch time but no room. Palais des Congres workshops are told to use hall Ternes; there is no equivalent for us. We should ask whether lunch is provided at Jussieu at all.
  • Can the same posters stay up for both sessions? SLM-Agents has Caves Esclangon between our two slots, so the 40 boards are likely cleared and reassigned. If so, presenters must mount twice and should be told in the acceptance email.
  • The afternoon break starts before coffee is served. Group A coffee runs 14:30 to 15:30, but this draft opens the session at 14:15. The poster room is available from 13:30, so the first 15 minutes would be posters without refreshments. Starting at 14:30 would align the two.
  • The demo track talk now has a 30 minute slot (15:30 to 16:00). The earlier plan was 10 minutes of talk plus 10 minutes of questions. Confirm the length we want to give Joydeep.
  • Lightning talks are afternoon only in this draft, with 30 minutes. They could be split across both halves if we shorten the panel.
  • Capacity is 120 with 35 reserved registrations. Worth comparing against the attendance survey before we publish.
